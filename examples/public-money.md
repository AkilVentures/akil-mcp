# Example: public money

Use this workflow when someone wants to follow awards, payments, contracts,
budgets, capital projects, or government funding tied to an organization,
agency, district, or official.

## Prompt

```text
Show public funding, contracts, and payments tied to this organization.
```

## Best anchors

Start with one of:

- organization name or EIN
- agency name
- council district or other boundary
- fiscal year or time window

## Useful record groups

- award history
- city payments
- contracts and solicitations
- agency budget and spending summaries
- council discretionary funding
- capital projects
- state or federal award records where relevant

## Good answer shape

```text
Separate awards, contracts, and payments.
Show the time window.
Call out the source checked for each record group.
Do not collapse possible fuzzy matches into facts.
```

## Caveats

An award, contract registration, and payment are different public-record events.
They should not be treated as the same thing.
